About Me

I am a postdoctoral researcher and Jang Young Sil Fellow (Excellence Track) at KAIST, working with Uichin Lee in the Interactive Computing Lab.

My goal is to advance human–computer interaction and build human-centered AI that understands people and that people can understand. I combine multimodal learning, models of human behavior, and human-centered evaluation to study AI across mental health, everyday behavior, and online social interaction.

My current interests follow two connected directions. (1) Longitudinal personalization: I study how personal AI can learn from everyday records, maintain and update memory, and adapt as people change.

(2) User simulation for agent evaluation and learning: I am interested in user digital twins and behavior-grounded simulation for evaluating and training personal agents. This includes reinforcement-learning environments informed by longitudinal user data, as well as representations that users can inspect and revise.

I received my Ph.D. in Artificial Intelligence from Hanyang University, advised by Kyungsik Han. From August 2019 to February 2020, I was a visiting scholar in the PIKE Lab at Pennsylvania State University, working with Dongwon Lee.
